About Zarou'Studios

Zarou'Studios is based in Ontario, Canada. Our work brings together sketches of flowers, plants, figures, and everyday objects — quiet studies that explore the space between observation, memory, and imagination.

The owner and founder of Zarou'Studios, Zara Saleh, is currently a high school student. Her recent work includes sketchbook drawings of flowers, plants, figures, and everyday objects, exploring her broad imagination.
